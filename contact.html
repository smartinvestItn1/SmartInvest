Frequently Asked Questions
How do I deposit money into my SmartInvest account?
▼

Go to your Dashboard and click the "Deposit" button. Choose your preferred payment method (bank transfer, card, or crypto), enter the amount, and follow the on-screen instructions. Most deposits are credited instantly. If you experience any delays, please contact our support team.

How long do withdrawals take to process?
▼

Withdrawals are typically processed within 24–48 hours. Once approved, funds will reach your bank account or wallet within 1–3 business days depending on your payment provider. VIP members enjoy same-day withdrawal processing.

Is my money safe with SmartInvest?
▼

Absolutely. We use bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption, two-factor authentication (2FA), and cold storage for digital assets. Your funds are held in segregated accounts with regulated financial institutions. We also conduct regular third-party security audits.

Can I change my investment plan after signing up?
▼

Yes! You can upgrade or switch your investment plan at any time from your Dashboard. Changes take effect immediately, and our support team can help you choose the best option based on your financial goals and risk appetite.

What should I do if I forget my password?
▼

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email. For security, the reset link expires after 1 hour. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

How does the referral program work?
▼

Share your unique referral code from your Dashboard. When someone signs up and makes their first deposit, you earn a commission on their investment activity. There's no limit to how many people you can refer — the more you invite, the more you earn.
