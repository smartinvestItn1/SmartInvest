Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you start

How do I deposit funds?
You can deposit via bank transfer, debit/credit card, or cryptocurrency (USDT). All deposits are processed instantly and there are zero deposit fees. Simply log in, go to your wallet, and select your preferred payment method.
How long do withdrawals take?
Most withdrawals are processed within 2 to 24 hours. Bank transfers may take 1-3 business days depending on your bank and country. Crypto withdrawals are typically completed within minutes. We do not charge withdrawal fees.
Is my investment capital protected?
Yes. SmartInvest operates under UK financial regulations and maintains segregated client accounts. Your capital is never mixed with company funds. We also carry comprehensive insurance coverage for all client assets held on our platform.
Can I withdraw my profits daily?
Absolutely. Profits are credited to your account daily and you can withdraw them at any time. There are no lock-in periods or minimum withdrawal amounts. You have full control over your money.
What is the minimum investment amount?
Our Starter Plan begins at just $100. This makes SmartInvest accessible to everyone, whether you are investing for the first time or building a diversified portfolio across multiple plans.
Do you offer customer support?
Yes, our UK-based support team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week via live chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, and email. Average response time is under 5 minutes.
